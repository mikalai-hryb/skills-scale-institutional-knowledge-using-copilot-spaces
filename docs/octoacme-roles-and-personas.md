# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business requirements into actionable tasks and documentation. They serve as the bridge between technical and non-technical stakeholders, ensuring that development work addresses real business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business requirements
- Facilitate requirements-gathering sessions with stakeholders
- Translate business needs into user stories and acceptance criteria
- Maintain traceability between requirements and delivered features
- Identify and resolve ambiguities before work enters development

### Goals
- Ensure development efforts align with business objectives
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or incomplete requirements
- Accelerate shared understanding across technical and non-technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User story documentation and acceptance criteria definitions
- Backlog refinement sessions with Product Managers and Developers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provides detailed acceptance criteria and clarifies requirements during implementation
- **Product Managers:** Supports backlog refinement and prioritization with documented requirements
- **Project Managers:** Flags scope risks early and assists in dependency mapping

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers define user experiences, create prototypes, and establish usability criteria. They advocate for the end user throughout the product lifecycle, ensuring that delivered features are intuitive and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize findings into design decisions
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and user flows
- Define usability acceptance criteria for features
- Conduct usability testing and share findings with the team
- Maintain design system consistency across the product

### Goals
- Deliver experiences that are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs
- Reduce usability defects discovered late in delivery
- Promote a user-centered design culture within the team

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and prototype walkthroughs
- Usability test reports and design documentation
- Collaborative sessions with Product Managers during feature discovery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers:** Collaborates during feature discovery to align design decisions with product goals
- **Developers:** Provides design specifications and reviews implementation for fidelity and usability
- **Business Analysts:** Aligns user flows with documented business requirements

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the testing strategy and quality gate for each release. They coordinate test coverage, manage bug triage, and provide the final sign-off on release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the overall test strategy (manual and automated)
- Coordinate test case creation and coverage reviews
- Triage defects and work with Developers on prioritization and resolution
- Sign off on release readiness based on defined quality criteria
- Track and report on quality metrics over time

### Goals
- Prevent regressions and quality degradation across releases
- Increase automated test coverage to reduce manual effort
- Provide clear, evidence-based release readiness assessments

### Typical Communication
- Test plan and test results documentation
- Bug triage meetings with Developers
- Release readiness reports to Project Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborates on bug triage, root cause analysis, and test environment setup
- **Project Managers:** Reports on test progress and flags quality risks that affect release schedules
- **Release Manager:** Provides the quality sign-off required before deployments proceed

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, maintain release plans, and own rollback and mitigation procedures. They ensure all pre-release checks are complete and that releases proceed safely and on schedule.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release calendar and coordinate deployment windows
- Define and communicate release checklists and go/no-go criteria
- Coordinate with all stakeholders ahead of each deployment
- Own rollback and incident response procedures for failed releases
- Track post-release metrics and coordinate hotfix processes when needed

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal disruption
- Maintain a reliable, repeatable release process
- Reduce time to recovery when incidents occur

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment runbooks
- Go/no-go calls and deployment coordination meetings
- Post-release retrospective summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Aligns on code freeze dates, deployment steps, and rollback procedures
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Receives quality sign-off before proceeding with deployment
- **Project Managers:** Coordinates release timelines with overall project schedules
- **Product Managers:** Communicates release scope and feature availability

---

## Stakeholder / External Partner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and External Partners provide requirements, grant approvals, and supply feedback throughout the project. They represent business, customer, or partner interests and participate in milestone reviews.

### Responsibilities
- Provide high-level requirements and business context
- Review and approve milestone deliverables and key decisions
- Supply timely feedback on demos and prototypes
- Participate in milestone review meetings and sign-off events
- Communicate external constraints, deadlines, or regulatory requirements

### Goals
- Ensure delivered work meets business and customer expectations
- Maintain visibility into project progress and risks
- Provide clear direction to reduce ambiguity for the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Milestone review meetings and executive briefings
- Written approvals and feedback on deliverables
- Regular status communications from Project or Product Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers:** Primary point of contact for requirements and priority alignment
- **Project Managers:** Receives status reports and escalations; participates in risk reviews
- **Business Analysts:** Works closely to validate that documented requirements accurately reflect business intent
