# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation! This README provides a comprehensive overview of how OctoAcme organizes, plans, executes, and improves projects, along with links to detailed guides for each phase.

## Project Management Processes Summary

OctoAcme employs a structured, customer-first lifecycle spanning five phases: **Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective**. Each project begins by validating business needs, aligning stakeholders, and establishing clear success metrics and rationale. The planning phase breaks down deliverables into shippable increments, defines responsibilities, and prioritizes backlogs with an emphasis on risk management and measurable outcomes.

Execution is guided by clearly defined roles—Project Managers coordinate delivery and risk, Product Managers own value and measurement, Developers implement features and quality standards, and QA ensures rigorous validation. Regular ceremonies such as daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and milestone demos maintain alignment and transparency. Work moves through a visible project board from Backlog to Done, and all pull requests are peer-reviewed and pass CI checks.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout, including unit, integration, and smoke testing, security scanning, and manual QA when needed. Risks are proactively captured with escalation paths, and metrics such as velocity, burndown, and product KPIs are shared through dashboards. Releases follow checklists for validation, communication, and rollback, with post-release verification and incident playbooks supporting reliability.

Every project or milestone concludes with a retrospective, capturing learnings and actions for continuous improvement and knowledge sharing. This repeatable, collaborative approach ensures that OctoAcme's project management practices are transparent, consistent, and continuously evolving to support both onboarding and long-term team success.
